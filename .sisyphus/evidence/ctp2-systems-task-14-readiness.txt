# Task 14 readiness report: bounded CTP2 gameplay systems slice
# 2026-09-22T19:25:00-04:00

## Scope statement
This branch supports a bounded, server-owned gameplay systems slice suitable for public HTTP/SSE clients and deterministic tests. It does **not** claim full retail Call to Power II mechanic parity. Original CTP2 source was used to align ambiguous behavior where inspected, but runtime data is synthetic and intentionally limited.

## Included systems
- Runtime rules import/compile for synthetic CTP2 units, advances, governments, terrain improvements, movement metadata, and bounded effects.
- Public projection/SSE contracts for visible tiles, owned/visible units, cities, research, diplomacy/government/public works, bounded rules metadata, legal-action metadata, and stable event envelopes.
- Deterministic city economy, production queues/building completion, city yields, public works, terrain cleanup/terraform/improvement overlays, pollution clamping, and movement-cost effects.
- Advances, prerequisites, research progress, government unlocks/effects, diplomacy contact/proposals/responses/treaties, embassies, special actions, movement, armies, combat, transports, and lifecycle/restart persistence.
- Production AI that reads public projections and submits ordinary public commands through the gameplay gateway.
- Cross-system product gates that run three public-client scenarios twice clean and once through restart.

## Deferred/excluded systems
- Full retail CTP2 rules database parity, complete unit/advance/government catalogs, and complete AI strategy parity.
- 60-row campaign acceptance as a readiness signal. Row 09 was intentionally demoted; campaign-row expansion remains a later track.
- Row 08 and other provisional rows remain fail-closed unless they receive immutable observed artifacts in a later acceptance track.
- UI/browser automation; public API/SSE tests are direct HTTP/SSE tests.
